Inference and Sampling Estimators

Feb 2026 Statistics

In our recent analysis of Pernambuco health data, we focused on population totals. When using a sample \( s \), the **Horvitz-Thompson** estimator for the total is defined as:

\[ \hat{Y}_{HT} = \sum_{i \in s} \frac{y_i}{\pi_i} \]

Probability Inclusion

For a given element \( i \), we define the first-order inclusion probability as \( \pi_i = P(i \in s) \). This ensures that our estimator is unbiased, satisfying the condition \( E[\hat{Y}_{HT}] = Y \).

Variance Estimation

The variance for this estimator can be calculated using the following expression:

\[ V(\hat{Y}_{HT}) = \sum_{i=1}^{N} \sum_{j=1}^{N} (\pi_{ij} - \pi_i \pi_j) \frac{y_i}{\pi_i} \frac{y_j}{\pi_j} \]

Where \( \pi_{ij} \) represents the second-order inclusion probability.
